# Journal Templates Documentation

This document describes the available journal templates in Lifestyle Spaces.

## Daily Gratitude

**Purpose:** Daily practice of gratitude and reflection

**Color:** Gold (#FBBF24)

**Cadence:** Daily

**Estimated Time:** 5-10 minutes

**Sections:**
1. Things I'm grateful for (Paragraph) - List gratitudes
2. Why they matter (Paragraph) - Reflect on meaning
3. Tomorrow's intention (Paragraph) - Set forward-looking intention

**Ellie Behavior:**
- Animation: zen, sway
- Messages: Welcome → Reflection → Celebration
- Particles: Hearts, Sparkles

---

## Weekly Gratitude & Reflection

**Purpose:** Deep weekly synthesis of meaningful moments and emotional themes

**Color:** Gold (#FBBF24)

**Cadence:** Weekly (suggested: Sunday evening)

**Estimated Time:** 15-20 minutes

**Sections:**
1. Meaningful Moments & Connections (List) - Capture week's highlights
2. Savoring an Experience (Q&A Pairs) - Deep dive into one moment
3. The Emotional Landscape (Paragraph) - Synthesize overall feeling
4. Cultivating Joy (List) - Forward-looking intentions

**Ellie Behavior:**
- Animation: calm-breathing (fallback for future proud-reflective-zen)
- Messages: Welcome → Encouragement → Celebration
- Particles: Sparkle (Section 2), Heart (completion)

**Design Rationale:**
- Gold differentiates from Daily Gratitude (yellow)
- Literal titles reduce cognitive load vs. metaphorical
- Progressive structure: List → Deep → Synthesize → Forward
- No progressive disclosure (all sections immediately accessible)

**Future Enhancements:**
- proud-reflective-zen animation state
- Weekly comparison feature
- Streak tracking
- Email reminders

## Template Design Guidelines

### Color Palette
- Use distinct colors to help users quickly identify templates
- Ensure sufficient contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA minimum)
- Color meanings:
  - Gold: Gratitude, appreciation, warmth
  - Blue: Calm, daily reflection
  - Purple: Emotions, feelings
  - Green: Growth, progress
  - Teal: Transformation, planning
  - Lavender: Calm, mindfulness
  - Orange: Action, decisions

### Ellie Integration
- Templates should have clear Ellie guidance at template and section levels
- Use appropriate moods: zen, curious, happy, excited, celebrating, proud
- Particle effects: hearts (love/gratitude), sparkles (celebration), treats (rewards)
- Timing: 2-3 second delays for onStart messages to avoid overwhelming

### Section Types
- **Paragraph:** Free-form text, good for reflections
- **List:** Bullet points, good for capturing multiple items
- **Q&A Pairs:** Structured questions and answers, good for deep dives
- **Checkbox:** Task lists, good for goals and habits
- **Scale:** Numerical ratings, good for tracking metrics
- **Table:** Structured data, good for comparisons
- **Time Log:** Time tracking, good for productivity

### Accessibility
- All templates must meet WCAG AA standards
- Provide clear section titles and prompts
- Ensure color contrast ratios meet minimums
- Support keyboard navigation
- Include aria-labels for screen readers
